# Changelog

## 1.0.60

### ⚙️ The Clockwork King has real mob AI
- **He walks.** He was a `setNoAi(true)` puppet nudged by hand every tick, which is why he hovered after his own Piston Slam and walked straight into walls. He is a normal mob now: vanilla pathfinding sends him round an obstacle instead of through it, and vanilla gravity brings him back down. His scripted attacks layer on top, unchanged.
- **Deliberately no melee goal.** The iron golem's default kit was cleared out first — its village-strolling goals would have walked him out of his own arena, and its 18-damage fist would have sat on top of an arsenal that is supposed to *be* his damage. What is left is what the fight wants: pick a player, walk at them, stop just outside reach.

### 🧹 Seven teardown hooks that nothing ever called
- **A whole class of "haunted arena" bug is fixed at the root.** Every boss and gear system had a `onServerStopping` teardown written for it, and **not one was registered with the server lifecycle**. So every world stop left the live fights' entities behind — bosses, machines, rigging, guards, summoned servants — while the in-memory state that drove them vanished with the process. What came back on the next boot was a body with no fight attached: no AI, no ticking, no loot. All seven are wired now, before the world is saved rather than after.
- The periodic stray sweeps added in 1.0.59 are a backstop for crashes now, not the only line of defence.

### 🩹 Fixes
- **Per-player combat state no longer leaks.** `CombatGear` — the oldest of the gear systems — released nothing when a player logged out, so it kept one entry per player who had ever landed a hit or charged a super jump, forever. It now releases its rows on disconnect and resets entirely on server stop, matching what every newer gear class already did.
- **`/changelog` was eleven releases stale.** It reads a bundled copy of the changelog, and that copy is now generated from `CHANGELOG.md` by the build instead of being maintained by hand.

### 🧪 Three new guards, because dead code compiles
- **`ffAuditSources` fails the build on an unwired shutdown hook.** The bug above was invisible for so long precisely because a teardown method nothing calls is perfectly legal Java. Proven to fail by unwiring one on purpose.
- **`bosses.clockwork-ai`** asserts the King's goal rig: movement present, player targeting present, melee and wandering absent, and that both AI accessor mixins actually apply.
- **`legendary.documented-numbers`** makes nine accessors load-bearing — they existed as "test hooks so the documented numbers cannot drift" and no test had ever called them. It now pins the Gauntlet's wind-up and ram reach, the Codex's spell cycle, Starfall and Void Reaver cadence, the Seal's cost, the Magister's overcharge window, and the Void Shaper's whole block-throwing table (stone, magma, ice, leaves, ore, log).
- **`changelog.current`** fails if the bundled in-game changelog is not the version the mod actually is.

## 1.0.59

### 🧱 The four new raid bosses are actually killable now
- **Their health is cut to endgame parity.** The Clockwork King, Starbound Magister, Void Shaper and Emerald Sovereign shipped at 1200 / 900 / 1400 / 1100 against the Scarlet Devil's 900, which is what "they just have WAY too much health" was. They are now 700 / 620 / 760 / 680.
- **The shields no longer multiply it.** The Clockwork King's machinery was worth up to a **four-tier Resistance** and the Sovereign's court up to **three** - an 80% damage cut, permanently reapplied while anything lived, which turned a 1200 HP boss into more than 6000 effective HP. Both cap at **one** tier now: breaking the arsenal or the court still matters, but the armour is never a wall.
- **The Sovereign heals less.** His Royal Tribute banked 12 HP per player taxed; with a full court in range he could out-heal a group's damage. It banks 5.

### 🎁 They always drop their loot
- **One loot path, paid exactly once.** Loot was granted only from the death ceremony, so any death that found another route - a command, an admin `/kill`, vanilla's own body falling over - dropped nothing at all. All four bosses now pay through a once-only ledger that the entity-death hook also calls: whichever route runs first pays, and the other is a no-op.
- **The ceremony can no longer be interrupted into a loss.** It used to mark the boss invulnerable to protect the sequence, which meant a crash or restart mid-ceremony left him **permanently unkillable** - alive, invulnerable, at 1 HP, with no fight to drive him. It now cancels blows instead, so a boss can never be left immortal.
- **Bodies orphaned by a crash are swept up.** Because a fight lives in memory, a boss saved when the server died comes back as a statue that never moves, never dies and never drops. A periodic sweep clears any boss body with no fight attached.

### ⚙️ The Clockwork King walks now
- **He no longer flies.** He is a no-AI puppet, so vanilla never applied gravity to him and never walked him anywhere: the Piston Slam puts him six blocks in the air and he simply stayed there, which made him a boss you could not reach from the ground. He is driven by hand now - grounded every tick, closing to just inside melee range - and rooted only while a telegraphed move is charging.

### 🧪 Two new self-test assertions
- **`bosses.new-are-killable`** - no new boss may out-tough the Scarlet Devil, and no shield may stack past one tier.
- **`bosses.new-loot-once`** - the loot ledger must survive being called with nothing to pay and nobody to pay it to, because the death hook calls it on every entity death and a throw there would be swallowed and silence the payout.

## 1.0.58

### 🎨 Art the new bosses and their loot were missing
- **Evoker and Illusioner cloaks and spellbooks redrawn.** All four are new 16x16 art, so the Evoker's and Illusioner's sets read as their own gear instead of a recoloured blur.
- **Excalibur drawn.** The duel roll's 1-in-1000 legendary finally has art of its own rather than looking like the netherite sword it is underneath.
- **The Mystery Box and all four Mystery Keys drawn.** The Box rendered as a plain barrel and every key tier rendered as the *same* tripwire hook - a Legendary Key and a Common Key were indistinguishable. Each tier has its own model now.
- **The Clockwork King's trophy and gauntlet redrawn** so the two pieces of his kit are no longer near-identical brass shapes.

### 🔮 The Illusioner's Spellbook does what it says
- **True invisibility.** The caster is hidden with the entity flag rather than the potion effect, so the particles they give off stay visible while the body does not. **Armour is hidden from other players, not removed** - an empty equipment packet is sent, so full netherite still protects exactly as it did. Mobs drop the caster's target and are refused new ones, so armour no longer widens the range you are spotted at.
- **Illusions update.** Every copy re-wears the caster's *current* kit each tick, so swapping a weapon or chestplate mid-window changes every clone with you instead of leaving them standing in the armour you cast in.

### 🧪 Each of the four new bosses has its own enchant
- **Overclock** (Clockwork King) - hits discharge an arc that overloads the target and drags in whatever is packed around it with Weakness and Slowness.
- **Starfall** (Starbound Magister) - the blow lands heavier and a star falls on the victim, cleaving the enemies around them.
- **Voidrend** (Void Shaper) - hits tear a seam through armour and drag the target *in* rather than knocking it away, the opposite of Seismic.
- **Sovereign's Levy** (Emerald Sovereign) - hits mark the target for tribute and the kill shakes emeralds loose.
- Each drops as a Tome from its own boss and fuses in the Item Forge like every other enchant: `/ff give overclocktome`, `starfalltome`, `voidrendtome`, `levytome`.

### 🩹 Fixes
- **The Starbound Magister dropped nothing.** Her lethal-blow hook was returning "allow the killing blow", so she died instantly and the death ceremony - and with it `grantLoot` - never ran. The same bug was in the Clockwork King, Void Shaper and Emerald Sovereign; all four are fixed.
- **The Sovereign's Bell now makes villagers fight.** Vanilla villagers have no attack goal, so buffing them left them standing there being hit. A rung bell turns nearby villagers into Royal Subjects that close on the nearest hostile and land a real pitchfork strike, credited to the villager.
- **The Shaping Sigil no longer throws blocks at you.** Gripping and hurling were the same click, so looking at a wall picked a block up and flung it. Right-click grips, sneak-right-click hurls.
- **The Void Reaver works.** It threw whatever you were looking at, which in a fight is the enemy and never terrain - so its whole gimmick was dead exactly when you were using it. It finds its own ammunition beside the victim now.
- **Excalibur was a dead item.** The self-test's right-click probe caught it with neither a handler nor a passive declaration; its 255 Strength and Sharpness live in a held-item tick, and it is declared passive now.

## 1.0.57

### ⛓ Prison is now The Cell Block, and it has a second axis
- **Heat.** Rich ore does not come for free any more. Every ore you mine adds Heat - nothing for stone, a point or two for coal and iron, five for diamond, ten for ancient debris - and it cools on its own, a point every second and a half. Past **45 Heat** the cell block notices you.
- **Guards.** They spawn in your tunnels and hunt you, with bounty, health and damage scaling off your rank, and they get more numerous and quicker to arrive into full **lockdown** at 75. Put one down for a cash bounty, Heat **-15**, and progress on the Guard contract. Your record going quiet at 20 Heat calls the whole sweep off.
- **You cannot die in here.** A lethal blow is intercepted exactly like an expedition fatal blow: you are dragged to **Solitary**, a real 5x5 boxed cell high above the mine, for 15 seconds of Blindness, Slowness and Mining Fatigue. Your mined haul is confiscated; your **stashed gear is untouched**, so nothing a player owns can be lost. It is a setback, not a wipe.
- **Contracts.** Three jobs sit on the noticeboard in the Prison Menu - mine ore, slay inmates, take down guards, or sell a haul - each paying bonus cash and refreshing when claimed. Reroll the board for cash if you do not like the work.
- **Break-out.** Every now and then a **loose brick** works free of your mine wall. Break it and a 26-block tunnel opens with guards flooding in behind you and a glowstone exit at the far end: reach it inside 45 seconds and you walk free with a jackpot, a Heat wipe and a line in the newspaper. Fail, and the tunnel is sealed back **block for block** and your Heat jumps 25.
- **The Prison Menu is a control board now.** Six rows showing your Heat meter, the guards currently on you, break-out status, all three contracts with progress bars, and the usual sell / rank / upgrade / convert / travel buttons. Selling launders Heat, so the haul you were carrying is also the thing that gets the dogs off you.
- **A fishing pond that paid nothing.** `onFishCatch` existed, was documented as paying $30-$80 a catch, and was **never called from anywhere** - the pond was decorative. It is hooked to the reel-in now, jackpot catches included.
- **An arena exploit that printed money.** The wave ticker ran for every player in the prison regardless of where they were, so starting the arena and walking back to the mine left an empty arena "clearing" wave after wave and paying the full bonus each time, forever. Waves now only advance while you are actually standing in the arena, leaving abandons the run, and no free payout is issued.

### ☄ Expeditions are rolled, not repeated
- **Anomalies.** Every run opens with one, announced on arrival and echoed in the action bar: **Rich Veins** (ore pays double), **Blood Moon** (kills pay double, and there are far more of them), **Gilded Caches** (chests pay double), **Unstable Reality** (collapses 30% sooner, everything pays half again), or plain **Stable Reality**. Two trips into the same site no longer play the same way.
- **Site events** every 30-60 seconds: **ORE RUSH** seeds a real vein in the floor near you, **SUPPLY DROP** lands a rich loot chest, **SWARM** floods the halls, **SENTINEL** sends a mini-boss, and **BLACKOUT** takes your sight away and lets the dark do the rest. Each announces itself in chat and in the action bar.
- **A run summary card.** Mobs slain, ores mined, chests opened, distance explored, minutes survived, your anomaly - and a **C/B/A/S rating** against the site's expected haul, with a real bonus paid on A and S. The card also prints on an empty-handed escape, because knowing the run was worth nothing is still information.
- The action bar now carries anomaly, threat tier, the active event and the escape hint alongside loot and time.

### 🧪 Three new self-test assertions
- No contract can pay for work that was not done, a contract slot that does not exist cannot be claimed, and rerolling is never free.
- A survivable hit inside prison is never intercepted, so a scratch cannot jail you and confiscate your ore.
- Every anomaly has a name, a description, non-zero loot multipliers and a timer multiplier that never extends a run.
- The fishing mixin originally injected a non-returning callback into `retrieve`, which **failed to apply and broke server startup outright**. The self-test caught it before it shipped.

## 1.0.56

### 🛠 Four new raid bosses, with their full item sets
- **⚙ The Clockwork King** - called by a **Clockwork Core**. His armour *is* his machinery: every live turret, blade, piston and drone is worth a tier of Resistance, and tearing one off chips his real health and opens a window. Kill the arsenal or hit a wall. Drops his Loot Box, Trophy, **Mech-Scrap**, the **Clockwork Gauntlet** (every 4th landed hit winds Overdrive and drives a piston ram through whatever is in front), the **Mechanical Heart** (self-repair that only ticks while you are *not* being hit) and **Automaton Armor** (plating plus fireproofing).
- **✨ The Starbound Magister** - called by an **Astral Compass**. A wandering fight with **no arena at all**: she keeps her distance and blinks away when you close. From 60% she banks Astral Energy - each point widens, quickens and hardens her spells - and at six she **overloads**, detonating it all and dropping her guard for five seconds. So the fight rewards interrupting her instead of out-poking her. At 25% she goes to Starfall: marked meteors, a Gravity Well, an Astral Dash, and **Celestial Collapse**, a telegraphed last spell she is spent after. Drops her Loot Box, Trophy, **Magical Essence**, the **Starpiercer**, the **Astral Mantle** and the **Magister's Codex** (three real spells: Star Bolt, Gravity, Meteor).
- **🟯 The Void Shaper** - called by a **Void Anchor**. He does not shoot fireballs: he rips blocks out of the world as floating displays and hurls them, and **the block decides the blow** - stone is heavy with huge knockback, magma burns, ice slows, ore flies fast, logs throw you far, leaves shatter into fragments. Ender Pull, Block Wall, Ender Step and Void Slam round him out, and at 40% he hoists five to eight blocks into orbit and **loses control** of all of them at once. Drops his Loot Box, Trophy, **Voidsteel Scrap**, the **Void Reaver**, the **Colossus Plate** and the **Shaping Sigil**.
- **👑 The Emerald Sovereign** - called by the **Sovereign's Crown**. He fights with subjects: a **Bell Toll** that staggers you and slows you, **Royal Decrees** that summon **Royal Guards**, a **Royal Tribute** that taxes vitality (never items), and at 50% **Sovereign's Judgement**, a beam you have to walk out of. His court is his armour - clear it and he opens up. Drops his Loot Box, Trophy, **Royal Tribute**, the **Royal Contract** (summons a real Royal Guard that fights for you), the **Sovereign's Bell** (staggers nearby hostiles, empowers nearby villagers) and the **Emerald Seal** (cheaper trades, emeralds from hostile kills, and a Royal Tribute buff for five emeralds).
- All four have custom item art, item definitions, model data, crafting recipes and recipe advancements, and appear in **/ff give**, **/raidboss help** and the boss codex names.
- **⚙ The King is built out of blocks.** Twelve **block displays** bolt his armour onto him - an iron visor and a lamp for an eye, an iron chestplate over a glowing core, deepslate shoulder plates on iron brackets, back plating, a hip carriage and iron legs - laid out in *his* frame so the whole suit turns with him. The suit is gated on the same number as his Resistance shield, so **stripping his machines visibly blows his armour off plate by plate**, and when he rebuilds he bolts it back on. His machines get chassis blocks too (dispenser, trapdoor, piston head, observer) riding them, so a turret reads as a turret instead of as a shulker. None of it has a hitbox or deals damage - it is the state of the fight made visible. Plating is tagged, so an orphaned piece left by a crash is swept up within 15 seconds instead of standing over an empty field forever.

### 🩹 Fixes
- **Art for every new item is real.** 28 new textures plus their model JSONs, and all seven loot-box families' dispatch entries are generated, so none of them render as the vanilla item they are built on.
- **A loot box that did nothing on right-click.** The four newest boxes were being swallowed by the boss-gear branch before the loot-box branch could see them, so clicking one returned `PASS` - dead in the hand. The self-test's right-click probe caught it.
- **No-AI bosses now actually move.** A no-AI puppet never runs its own physics step, so driving one with `setDeltaMovement` leaves it hovering in place; the Magister and the Void Shaper are moved by position each tick, exactly like the Clockwork machines.
- **Leaves can be thrown.** The void-shaping eligibility test used `isSolidRender`, which is false for leaves - so the documented "leaves shatter into fragments" attack was unreachable. It now tests the *collision* shape, which is what "a full block" actually means.

## 1.0.55

### ⏳ The Pocket-Watch cannot stop a Time Lord
- **He refuses it, and then proves the point.** Against him the watch no longer freezes the world: the tick freeze is dropped, no player is pinned, and the bubble only stills the **mobs** around you. He walks straight through it, laughs - *"You stopped time with a trinket?"* - and a beat later answers with *"Let me show you how it is done"*, stopping time himself so you can watch a real stop from the wrong end of it.
- Everyone else in the bubble is left alone too, so nothing about the rest of the fight pretends to be paused.
- **A refused watch is a quiet one.** Three things followed from the same mistake - that a bubble he walked through was still a stopped moment. His blows were being *banked* and handed back as a single combined hit (the exact burst the bank exists to prevent), arrows that never stopped moving were getting the i-frame bypass, and the stop's "time resumes" stinger fired while his own stop was still running. None of it happens now: only a stop that actually holds the world banks damage, only a stop that is really holding projectiles tags them, and a refused watch ends silently. The bubble also outlasts his retort, so its cleanup can no longer wake the mobs while he is still holding them.

### 🎁 Loot boxes pay the people who earned them
- **The Scarlet Devil and Time Lord boxes go straight into the inventory of every player who fought**, instead of a single box on the floor for whoever happened to be standing nearest when the body dropped. Each participant gets a delivery line telling them how many arrived.

### 🩸 A Blood Revenant takes nothing from you
- **It wears copies. You keep your gear.** The servant used to confiscate your whole inventory so `/claim loot` could hand it back, and that contract kept breaking - it fought Nice Keep Inventory, split stacks with the grave system, and made you kill your own corpse to get your tools back. Now it loots *nothing*: it wears a look-alike of your armour and weapon, **drops nothing**, and whatever you were carrying stays with you. It works with Nice Keep Inventory on or off.
- **A claimed death no longer swallows anything - this one was losing inventories.** The claim cancelled *every* drop so the servant could carry the real stacks. Once the servant stopped carrying them, that cancellation had nothing left to give them back, so a player killed in the King's blood phase or by the Scarlet Devil silently lost their **entire** inventory. The hold is gone: a claimed death is now an ordinary death, so Nice Keep Inventory keeps what it keeps and the rest falls where it fell.
- **The servant still looks like the person it was raised from.** Vanilla clears each slot the instant it has dropped it, so the kit is now captured at the very first drop of the death - the last moment the whole inventory exists - and the servant dresses from that. The capture is cosmetic and never writes back, so it cannot duplicate or consume a real item.

### ☠️ The Wither, harder and cleaner
- **The nuke is a real storm: ~40 skulls per player**, plus a carpet around the boss so the blast zone is not a safe spot. Supercharged and King variants scale it higher again.
- **Wither wreckage always rebuilds.** Every block it carves now enters the shared Explosion Rebuild queue **unconditionally** - not gated behind the `Explosion Rebuild` toggle, which previously meant an admin turning player explosions off also condemned every arena the Wither had visited to permanent craters.

### ⚔️ Raid art remade, player raids readable
- **Every raid texture is redrawn** - the loot box, the raiders' upgrader, the Warlord's Cloak and Trophy - while the **Warlord's Axe** and the **Captain's Horn** are left exactly as they were.
- **A live raid readout in the action bar**, once a second: which wave you are on out of how many, how many raiders are still standing, then how many bosses remain and how long the fight has run. The *"no defenders"* countdown rides the same line, so the one piece of raid state nobody can afford to miss is impossible to miss.
- **Broadcasts are scoped to the raid's own dimension.** The abandonment warning used to be server-wide, telling a player mining two dimensions away about a raid they had never seen.

## 1.0.54

### 🩸 Her three legendaries actually work
- **The Grimoire speaks in the action bar.** Cast, cooldown, spell-switch and "nothing in reach" all report to the overlay bar, so cycling spells mid-fight no longer floods chat.
- **Its spells land.** Blood Spears resolved its target through the *player* list only, so aiming at a mob cancelled the entire volley on the next tick - it now finds anything living by id. Blood Rain only looked for `Monster`s, so over a field of ordinary mobs it drained nothing; it now drinks from every living thing in range that is not yours. Bloodsuck falls back to the nearest target when the crosshair is a degree off.
- **The bats are a swarm.** Night Swarm's bats were ordinary bats with no owner and no target, so they flapped off into the night. Each one now hunts the caster's nearest enemy, bites it, and drifts back to the caster when the field is clear.
- **The Blood Prism is a ritual duel.** Channelling now roots you where you stand: move or take a single hit and the channel breaks. The drain pulses the target's health away, and if their health reaches zero they die there and then.
- **A player killed by the ritual owes nothing.** Their inventory is copied before the kill and written straight back - the death is real, the walk back is real, and no item is lost. The servant that rises wears only a *copy* of their armour, holds no gear, and drops nothing.
- **A Revenant can never bite its owner.** Its vanilla goals could set the wrong target between our ticks; a damage gate now refuses any hit from a servant or bat aimed at its own master, and the tick loop clears such targets outright.
- **Servants are named for who they were.** "Blood Revenant of <victim>", mobs included, so a raised cow is unmistakable.

### ⏳ A frozen volley lands as a volley
- **Pocket-Watch projectiles ignore invulnerability frames.** Everything hanging in the bubble is tagged while time is held, and those arrows punch through the 10-tick i-frame window as they land - stop time, empty a quiver, and every arrow counts.

## 1.0.53

### 🩸 The Scarlet Devil, finished
- **Three legendary weapons, not one.** Her loot box now rolls exactly three legendaries - the **Scarlet Fang** (a real diamond spear: it thrusts, and every hit drinks a share of the damage), the **Scarlet Grimoire** (casts her own move set; sneak to cycle spells) and the **Blood Prism** (bleeds a mob *or a player* into a blood servant of yours). Her Trophy and the Bloodsoaked Core stay out of the legendary pool, because a trophy and a forge material are not weapons.
- **One forge material instead of two.** The Crimson Essence did the same job as the Bloodsoaked Core and only made the drop table confusing; she drops Cores only now.
- **Her health bar actually moves.** It was created at summon time and never once told what her health was, so it sat full no matter how much she bled or drank. It is driven every tick now.
- **She no longer heals too much.** Her bite drinks a small capped share of the bite, the blood rain drinks a capped share of what it drained, and neither can hand her a whole health segment.
- **She no longer hides inside blocks.** The unstick ran only in the baseline-movement branch, so the swoop - the one move that drives her through terrain - could park her inside a wall until the fight happened to reach that branch. She is unstuck every tick and mid-swoop.
- **Blood Revenants give your gear back.** A servant captured its victim's gear from the *live* inventory, but by the time a death is reported vanilla has already emptied every slot - the only surviving copy is the keep-inventory snapshot, which the death handler deliberately leaves for the hand-off. Servants now take their gear (armour, held items and all) out of that snapshot, so `/claim loot` is never empty, and a servant left behind by a despawned boss still hands everything back.
- **The "she can't be summoned again" state is gone.** A fight whose phantom had already vanished is swept on the next summon instead of blocking the summoner forever.
- **Her textures and models render.** The item definitions that make a chest / glass bottle / diamond spear draw *her* art had been deleted from the pack; they are regenerated, and the pack ships them.

### ⏳ The Time Lord's finale, action bar and free launches
- **Stop, in the action bar.** Everyone caught in a time stop now sees a live countdown in their action bar for its whole duration.
- **His 1% finale.** At the last percent he stops trading blows, floats clear and **glows** (so you can find him through terrain), then walks a thick aging beam across every player in turn. Fail to land one clean hit on him in time and the years catch up all at once: you fall where you stand and a skeleton wearing your name takes your place. Land a hit and you are released instantly. Either way the fight then resolves - he is spent, and his death ceremony (with its loot) plays out.
- **Being launched no longer costs you the fall.** Every move of his that throws a player now marks them, and their fall damage is zeroed until they land.
- **The Pocket-Watch is a real stop now.** It uses the server tick freeze, so arrows and thrown items **hang in mid-air** and resume when time does, while the wielder walks through the frozen moment. Everyone else in the bubble is pinned. And **every point of damage dealt inside it is banked**, then delivered in one blow when the stop lifts - you can no longer be bursted down during your own watch.

### ☠️ The Wither, reworked properly
- **Skull Rain works.** Its meteors were cast at the world-surface heightmap, so in any arena that was not under open sky they spawned inside the ceiling and died there. They now spawn in the lowest *open* column above their target and arrive as a real barrage.
- **The Wither Nuke is a skull storm.** Instead of a single blast at its own body, the sky opens and skulls rain across the whole arena - around each player and around the boss - so standing still is the only way to be hit by all of them. The explosion itself is smaller now, because the storm is the damage.
- **The skull shield no longer breaks the fight.** It used to park the boss in place for ten seconds and left the health bar frozen (vanilla only writes bar progress from its own AI step). It is five seconds, it moves normally through it, and the bar is refreshed by hand.
- **Its loot box is guaranteed.** Whatever path ends the death animation, the rewards are now granted - a death captured by `/kill` or an interrupted sequence used to vanish with no loot at all.
- **Two new moves:** **Grave Burst** (a forward cone of skulls) and **Wither Wail** (a rooted, withering scream with darkness on top), and the nuke is no longer phase-2-only, so a normal fight can actually see it.

### 📦 Item art
- **Every custom item definition is back in the pack.** The generated `assets/minecraft/items/*.json` overrides had been removed from the working tree, which is why the Time Lord's summon item, legendaries and loot box, and the Scarlet Devil's whole set, kept rendering as the vanilla items they are built on. Regenerated, included in the mod jar, and the resource-pack zip rebuilt with the current metadata.

## 1.0.52

### ⏳ The Time Lord's time stop is a real stop now
- **The world freezes, and you do too.** The stop already froze the server tick rate, which is what hangs mobs and projectiles mid-air - but players are client-driven, so they kept walking and swinging. Every participant is now **pinned in place** for the length of the stop, mid-air players included, and cannot move, attack, use an item, open a block or mine while frozen.
- **He is not frozen with it.** The world stops, so his own AI would have too - a boss that stands still is a boss that does nothing. While time is stopped he now crosses the gap and strikes directly, so the stop is something he does *to* you rather than a pause he sits through.
- **The knockback lands when time resumes.** The damage you take inside a stop is banked, and the moment it releases you are shoved away from him, scaled by how much you were hit and hard-capped so it can never turn into a launch.
- **No more launching.** The old stop applied **Jump Boost 128**, which is not a "no jumping" flag on this version - it is a rocket. That is what threw players above the clouds mid-stop, and why the boss then despawned: they had left the arena. Player pinning replaces it, so a mid-air stop now holds you exactly where you were.
- **The arrows stopped one-shotting.** Volley damage was dialled back and the crit flag removed (arrow damage also scales with arrow speed, so the base is capped), and the phase-two ring now fires sixteen lighter arrows instead of twenty-four heavy ones.
- **More time to spend.** Three new moves join the rotation: **Chrono Bombs** (countdowns underfoot that go off together), an **Aging Ray** that hitscan-ages its target, and **Rewind** - below half health he once per fight steps back through his own timeline to heal and blink away.
- **He says each line once.** Identical lines inside a short window are dropped, and the death ceremony's two speeches announce exactly once each instead of repeating every ten ticks.

### 🎁 Loot boxes: the new boxes actually open
- **The Time Lord and Scarlet Devil Loot Boxes were missing from the menu's box-name and box-consume tables**, so opening one claimed *"You don't have a King Wither Skeleton Loot Box anymore"* - a box nobody had. ## 1.0.50

### 💥 Explosion Rebuild now covers the wreckage a boss makes
- **Boss craters used to land in a private list.** The wither's slam and nuke craters are carved one block at a time by `WitherReworkManager`, which kept its own `removeBlock` queue - invisible to everything else. So Explosion Rebuild covered TNT and `level.explode(...)`, but the wreckage a boss actually made rode a second, hidden queue. Boss carving now goes through the *same* rebuild queue as an explosion: same delay, same config switch, same restore path, same frozen columns - and a crash mid-fight cannot strand it any more.
- **A bare `/explosionrebuild` used to *flip the switch*.** Running it just to read the setting silently turned the feature off, which is exactly how "explosion rebuild is broken" gets reported. It now reports: ON or OFF, what triggers a rebuild, that it never touches claims, and how many blocks are queued right now. `/raidcooldown` and `/NiceKeepInventory claimother` had the same trap and now report too; `on` / `off` are the only things that change anything.
- **Proven, not assumed.** `ffSelfTest` gained `explosion.rebuild-boss-source`: it builds a stone shelf in the air, detonates a real `level.explode(zombie, ...)` next to it, and asserts that every block the blast removed was queued, was put back, that the queue drained, and that **no item entity dropped** (the duplication bug). It then runs a real wither carve and asserts that one queues and restores too. The build now fails if boss wreckage ever stops rebuilding again.

### 💥 Explosion Rebuild, take two - it was off, and my first proof was too weak
- **It defaulted to OFF.** A server that never opened the config had no blast recovery at all, so TNT, creepers, blood revenants and every kind of nuke left permanent holes. It is now **ON unless an admin turns it off** (matching raid cooldown and the wither rework), and if a config file explicitly says otherwise, startup logs one clear line saying so, with the command to change it.
- **My previous test proved the wrong half.** It used a test-only flush that skips the five-second delay, so it never exercised the code that actually runs in game. The check now ages the queue and then calls the **real tick**, and separately asserts that a blast is *not* queued while the feature is off - so a queue that captures but never drains now fails the build.
- If your `config.json` still says `explosion_rebuild: false` (a very likely leftover from the bare command that used to flip the switch), run `/explosionrebuild on` once - it will report the state before and after.

### 🤖 Duel bots no longer hit you through the ceiling
- **Their reach was 2D.** A bot's mace leap is a horizontal arc, and the landing smash was gated on horizontal distance alone - so a bot standing on the ground connected with a player eight blocks above it. The smash and the leap start now both check the vertical gap too, which is what a real player's reach does.
- **The mace smash was being eaten by the light-attack pacing window.** A single heavy falling hit was treated like the third click of a spam combo and discarded. A falling mace swing now always lands.

### 💨 Sonic Boom actually fires now
- **The trigger was too strict to ever register.** It demanded more than 1.7 blocks/tick *and* a standing elytra, but a firework glide sits right at that edge - which is exactly why it never seemed to work. Charging now starts above 0.5 blocks/tick of gliding and completes in 14 ticks (8 for follow-ups).
- **No more 60-second lockout.** The rule is now *once per flight*: after a boom, folding your wings or landing re-arms it, so a long trip can boom again and again - just never twice inside one glide. A 1.5s guard stops a double-fire.
- **It is genuinely faster than normal.** The launch now holds you at 3.4 blocks/tick along your look direction for about a second (a boosted firework glide is ~1.5-2.0), keeping a quarter of your old momentum so it cannot be aimed backwards, and it flags the client so the speed actually sticks. - **Every spawner was recorded as owned by the literal name "unbound".** The display fallback (`spawnerOwnerName` returns `"unbound"` when no name is stored) was being read by the record-writing path, so a spawner item carrying an owner UUID but no name re-bound itself with the placeholder - and the placeholder became the real owner name, permanently. The display form and the stored form are now separate methods, records are self-healed on load (owner known, name missing), and names are kept in a cache inside `spawners.json` that refreshes from the online players, so the repair survives restarts and works for owners who are offline.

### 🔨 The Item Forge could not upgrade anything it was supposed to

- **The Wither Loot Box rendered as the King's box.** Both are chest items and both claimed `custom_model_data 4550005`, so the resource pack could only ever resolve one of them. The Wither box now has its own id and its own ashen texture.
- **The Elder Warden Loot Box had no model entry at all** - it rendered as a plain chest. Added to the chest definition.

- **`ffAuditSources` now scrubs iCloud conflict copies first.** A resurrected `chair 2.json` inside `src/` used to fail the audit for the wrong reason.
- **The Vanilla clock dial is preserved.** The Clock's item definition dispatches on the in-game time; the override embeds the vanilla definition verbatim as its fallback, so ordinary clocks keep their dial. The overrides for all four new base items are generated by `tools/make_item_definitions.py`, and the new textures by `tools/make_time_lord_textures.py`, so the art and the dispatch tables are reproducible.

### 🧪 The mod now tests itself (run this before every release)

It fails on: any shipped datapack JSON that does not parse, any crafting recipe whose recipe-book **unlock advancement** is missing or vice versa (the recipe would be invisible in the book), any **menu class that no code opens** (an unreachable feature), and any command `/ff help` advertises that is not actually registered.

## 1.0.42

### 🧹 Floating popup leak fixed (the "Sticky!" stacking bug)
- **The bug**: proc popups ("§aSticky!", "§7Seismic!", "§c♥ +4", ...), possessed-mask price labels ("§a$12"), and grave name labels are real floating-text entities. They were spawned **without any marker**, so when the server crashed or was force-quit (before their 1.5s expiry sweep ran) they were saved into the world — and on reload nothing owned them anymore. - **A stale-display sweep runs at startup and every 5 minutes**, discarding:
  - tmp-tagged popups that the live systems no longer track (crash leftovers, and mask labels whose wearer logged out mid-glow — a second leak found while fixing this one);
  - tagged grave labels whose player-head skull is gone (e.g. removed with worldedit);
  - **untagged strays from older builds**, recognized by their exact old popup texts + the old spawn pattern, so your current world's existing pile gets cleaned without touching admin decor or vanilla displays.
- Live popups and mask labels are tracked in memory, so the sweep can never blink an actively-shown popup; if a mask-wearer returns to a mob whose orphaned label was swept, the mask-sense system now **adopts the surviving label** instead of stacking a duplicate.
- Sweep results are logged (`Discarded N stale floating-text display(s)`), so you can see how many were eating your TPS.

## 1.0.6

### Critical Fixes
- **1.8 combat 1-dmg bug fixed** — `LegacySweepMixin` rewritten. The old mixin wrapped `getAttackStrengthScale(0.5F)` and returned 0 to suppress sweep, but that return value also gates main-hit damage, causing the 1-dmg bare-hand punch. The new approach lets the damage scale run naturally (the +100 attack speed modifier already makes it 1.0) and instead suppresses sweep by returning an empty entity list from `getEntitiesOfClass` during legacy duels.
- **Anvil "Too Expensive" removed** — `AnvilMixin` caps repair cost at 39 (the vanilla hard cap) so items can always be combined as long as you have the XP.
- **Mining pity system** — `MiningPity`: breaking naturally-generated stone (3%: raw iron/copper/redstone/lapis), deepslate (4%: raw iron/gold/diamond/emerald), tuff (2%: raw iron), or netherrack (3%: gold nuggets/quartz/netherite scrap) has a chance to drop bonus raw ores.
- **Better multiplayer sleep** — `BetterSleepMixin`: only 50% of online players need to sleep to skip the night (rounds up, minimum 1).
